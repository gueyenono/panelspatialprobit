# panelspatialprobit: Bayesian Panel Spatial Probit and Ordered Probit Models

Bayesian Markov chain Monte Carlo estimation of spatial probit and
spatial ordered probit models for panel data. Supports spatial
autoregressive, spatial Durbin, spatial lag of X, spatial error and
combined specifications with time-invariant or time-varying weights;
pooled, random, spatially autocorrelated and correlated random effects;
time effects; dynamic models with lagged outcomes (Wooldridge initial
conditions) or latent autoregressive dynamics; normal or Student-t
errors; and impacts (direct, indirect and total marginal effects) on
outcome probabilities. Sampling builds on the data augmentation approach
of Albert and Chib (1993)
[doi:10.1080/01621459.1993.10476321](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1993.10476321)
, its spatial extension by LeSage (2000)
[doi:10.1111/j.1538-4632.2000.tb00413.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1538-4632.2000.tb00413.x)
and the panel models of Kakamu and Wago (2005).
