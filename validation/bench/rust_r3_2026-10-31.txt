== Notes ==
- GHK with 4 workers: 2.7-2.9x. Ten draws over 4 workers is at most 3 draws per worker
  (3.3x at best); the >= 3x target assumed threads over replications.
- psp_ppc(): the Moran's I and share statistics stay in R and take about half the time.
- Speed-ups found during the port, identical results: one LU per distinct W per draw for S and
  S' (faer's supernodal factor when it chooses one); probe columns that are not needed
  (impact columns for predict/PPC/response, a duplicate UK = UG) are not solved; the
  response-path sd is computed once per draw and weight matrix instead of per period and
  scenario; one-sided GHK intervals evaluate one normal tail.
